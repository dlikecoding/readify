Privacy Policy

Readify Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Readify YouTube channel.

What it uses

It uses YouTube API Services. With the owner's own Google sign-in, it uploads the channel's videos, sets their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists, and reads the channel's own statistics.

What it keeps

The sign-in key is stored only on the owner's computer. The tool collects no personal data from viewers or anyone else, shares nothing with anyone, sells nothing and shows no ads. This website uses no cookies and no tracking.

Google and YouTube

By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page.
